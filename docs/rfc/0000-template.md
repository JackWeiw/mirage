## Summary

## Motivation

- What problem does this solve? Who hits it, and what goes wrong today?
- Why now? What makes this the right time?

## Proposed design

The design in enough detail that a reviewer can evaluate it without re-deriving
it. Include:

- The shape of the change (new modules/files, changed data flows, new/changed
  APIs, config).
- How it fits the existing architecture (what it reuses, what it replaces).
- Key invariants and how they are preserved or changed.
- Non-goals (what this explicitly does NOT do).

## Alternatives considered

For each alternative: what it is, why it was rejected, and what it would take
to revisit. (Honest alternatives make the chosen design credible.)

## Risks / trade-offs

- What can go wrong? (correctness, performance, complexity, migration cost)
- How are the risks mitigated or accepted?

## Rollout / migration

- How does this land incrementally? (flags? a migration path? behind a new
  entry point?)
- What breaks for existing callers/tests?
